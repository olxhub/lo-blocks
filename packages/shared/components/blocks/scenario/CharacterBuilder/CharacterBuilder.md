# CharacterBuilder

Builds one character out of typed cards: dimension cards drawn from a catalog,
open-ended bio cards, and RPG-style stats cards with real units.

Attributes are generated from the schema and shown on this block's Overview tab.

## Why cards rather than a form

A form fixes what a character HAS. Cards let an author decide that per character —
one needs three dimensions and a bio, another needs stats — and lets them be
reordered, which is how a character sheet is actually written.
