## Repko's Broad Model of Interdisciplinary Research

According to Repko and colleagues (2019), a problem qualifies for interdisciplinary research when:

1. **Complexity** - The problem is too complex to be addressed by a single discipline
2. **Multiple Disciplines** - Different disciplines offer distinct insights into the problem
3. **Important** - The problem is significant and worth investigating
4. **Unresolved** - The problem has not been adequately solved

**IMPORTANT: Avoid Bias** - The researcher must avoid privileging any single disciplinary perspective
